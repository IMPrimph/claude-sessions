# Evertusk desktop shell

Scope: the whole desktop app (Operate). Audience, job and constraints live in PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A live departure board for your agent work. Find any past moment across tools in seconds. Refuses the folder-grid drill-down and the wall-of-tool-calls transcript.
OWN-WORLD: Fine neutral near-black scale with one amber meaning "look here"; red only for failure. System type for reading; condensed numerals for times and counts. Hairline-ruled rows on one strict grid. Light and bright themes keep the same roles.
STORY: You arrive with something in mind, search or scan the board, open the session, read a summary of what happened, follow your prompts and decisions, and unfold tool activity only when you need proof.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail 232px with search, tool filter, Saved and projects. Middle board 372px of fixed-column session rows, newest first. Right pane: title, facts, summary strip, tabs (Conversation, Changes, Agents, Stats), then the conversation with tool runs folded.
FORM: Fused Quiet Product and Departure Board: position 6 challenger, seed 0f171b0a. Signature interaction: rows and numbers flip into place when a running session changes, with amber lamps for running; plain update under reduced motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
